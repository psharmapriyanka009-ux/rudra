"# Primary System Requirements (Baseline)" 
"" 
"| ID      | Statement | Priority | Verification Method |" 
"|---------|-----------|----------|---------------------|" 
"| REQ-001 | Flight controller shall generate PWM signals to ESCs at 50 Hz with <= 2% jitter | Critical | Hardware-in-loop + scope |" 
"| REQ-002 | Attitude PID loops shall maintain roll/pitch stability within +/-2 degree under 15 m/s wind | Critical | Flight test + log analysis |" 
"| REQ-003 | ESC command-to-response latency shall remain <= 8 ms at all supported PWM frequencies | High | Oscilloscope + timing probe |" 
"| REQ-004 | LiPo battery pack temperature shall not exceed 60 C during continuous 100% throttle for 5 minutes | High | Thermal camera + BMS log |" 
"| REQ-005 | All requirement changes shall be executed only on an isolated Git branch; master baseline remains locked | Critical | Git history + review |"
