"# Change Request Impact Assessment" 
"**CR ID:** CR-2026-PWM-FREQ" 
"**Title:** Raise PWM Frequency from 50 Hz to 400 Hz" 
"**Branch:** cr/pwm-50hz-to-400hz" 
"" 
"## 1. Change Description" 
"- Current: 50 Hz" 
"- Proposed: 400 Hz" 
"" 
"## 2. Downstream Ripple-Effect Impact" 
"" 
"### 2.1 Attitude PID Gains (REQ-002)" 
"- High Impact: Existing gains become invalid at 8x rate" 
"- Kp increase, Kd increase, Ki decrease required" 
"- Full re-tune + wind-gust re-test mandatory" 
"" 
"### 2.2 ESC Latency (REQ-003)" 
"- CRITICAL Impact: Period becomes 2.5 ms" 
"- Latency budget collapses from 8 ms to < 2.5 ms" 
"- Many ESCs will fail. Prefer DShot/Oneshot" 
"" 
"### 2.3 LiPo Thermals (REQ-004)" 
"- Medium Impact: Higher switching losses increase heat" 
"- Soft limit should be lowered temporarily" 
"- Full thermal soak re-validation required" 
"" 
"## 3. Recommendation" 
"**STATUS: HOLD** until ESC hardware confirms 400 Hz support and PID + thermal re-validation is done." 
"No unauthorized modifications made on master branch."
